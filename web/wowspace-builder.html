WowSpace HeSuVi Builder

Dial in the sound of the old SRS WOW Thing Winamp plug-in (3D space, center level, TruBass, focus, headphone mode), hear it, then export it as a ready-to-install PipeWire output for Arch Linux, with a HeSuVi-format impulse file included.

Export for Arch Linux

The zip has two ways to install. Pick one: running both processes the sound twice.

Option A · EasyEffects preset

  1. Run bash install-easyeffects.sh from the unzipped folder. It puts the impulse in ~/.local/share/easyeffects/irs/ and the preset in ~/.local/share/easyeffects/output/ (and the Flatpak folders if you use the Flatpak).
  2. In EasyEffects open Presets → Output and load your preset. The chain is Bass Enhancer → Convolver → Limiter.
  3. The Bass Enhancer generates real bass harmonics, which is the part of TruBass an impulse file can't hold. Its amount and frequency follow your TruBass and Speaker size settings.

Option B · PipeWire output, no extra app

  1. Run bash install.sh. It copies the impulse files to ~/.config/pipewire/wowspace/, writes a filter-chain config to ~/.config/pipewire/pipewire.conf.d/ and restarts PipeWire. No root needed.
  2. Pick the new WowSpace output (named after your preset) in your sound settings or pavucontrol, or run wpctl status then wpctl set-default <id>.
  3. To remove it, delete its sink-wowspace*.conf and restart PipeWire.

Also in the zip. The 14-channel HeSuVi-format file, which works with PipeWire's sink-virtual-surround-7.1-hesuvi.conf example and with HeSuVi on Windows.

What carries over. The impulse reproduces every linear part of WOW: the 3D difference-signal curve, center level, focus and the TruBass resonance. Real TruBass also generated bass harmonics. Option A adds those with EasyEffects' Bass Enhancer; Option B, HeSuVi and this preview can't, so raise TruBass a little there to make up for it.
